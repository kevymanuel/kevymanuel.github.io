How I work

Build ecosystems, not features.

Every product should make the others more valuable. I design features and the business model together, so each one drives engagement, retention and growth across the whole system.

Bring everyone in early.

The best decisions happen when sales, engineering, design and leadership shape them together. I write those decisions down, so teams stay aligned long after the meeting.

Use AI where it earns its place.

AI is powerful, but it isn't the goal. I use it where it clearly helps the user or the business, and keep things simple everywhere else.

Prototype to learn.

I build working prototypes with Claude and Replit, then put them in front of real people before engineering starts.
